## Romanized (for reading off a teleprompter)

1. Zomato, Swiggy, Zepto… ee startups ki vandala kotlu ekkadi nunchi vastaayi? 6 stages lo cheptha.
2. Stage 1, Bootstrapping. Founders tama sontha savings tho start chestaaru. Konnisaarlu friends, family kuda help chestaaru.
3. Stage 2, Pre-seed. Modati bayati investment. Angel investors ikkada product kante founders ni nammi dabbu pedataaru.
4. Stage 3, Seed. Ippudu product undi. Prashna okkate: customers nijamga vaadutunnaaraa? Dabbulu kadutunnaaraa?
5. Stage 4, Series A. Edo work avutondi. VC firms vachi daanni pedda business ga maarchadaaniki dabbu pedataayi.
6. Stage 5, Series B, C, D. Kotha cities, kotha products. Prathi round lo amount inka perugutundi.
7. Stage 6, Exit. Investors ki dabbulu eppudu vastaayi? Company IPO ki vellinappudu, leda pedda company daanni konnappudu.
8. Kaani oka catch undi. Prathi round lo founder share taggutundi. Kaani company value perigithe, aa chinna share viluva chaala ekkuva.
9. Ee stage gurinchi detailed ga kaavaali? Comment cheyyandi. Ilaanti startup stories kosam Startup Hub Telugu ni follow cheyyandi!

## Filming checklist

- Phone camera at eye level, face near a window for light.
- Quiet room. Hold the phone 30 to 50 cm away so the audio is clear.
- Record each line as a separate clip; easier to redo mistakes.
- Cut every pause. Fast pace keeps people watching.
- Add subtitles in the Edits or CapCut app (most people watch muted).
- Music: soft instrumental at 10 to 15% volume under your voice.
- Cover image: first frame with "Startup money ఎక్కడి నుంచి? 💰".
