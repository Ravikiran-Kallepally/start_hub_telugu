## What it tells us

- Reels work for reach: 58x more people than the carousel, mostly strangers.
- Nobody interacted, so people watched briefly and scrolled on. The problem is now holding attention, not being seen.
- Likely causes: static slides with no movement, no subtitles (most people watch muted, so they missed the explanation), no face, no music, 62 seconds long.

## Changes for reel 002

- Burn in subtitles of every spoken line.
- Add movement (slow zoom, progress bar) so the video doesn't look static.
- Shorter: 30 to 40 seconds.
- Stronger hook in the first 2 seconds, ideally a surprising fact or number.
- Soft background music.
